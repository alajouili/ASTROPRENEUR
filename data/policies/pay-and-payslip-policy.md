# Pay and Payslip Policy (fictional company, 2026)

Content contributed by Member 3 (Compensation Handbook, section 5.1). Individual salaries and salary bands are not part of this document.

## Asking for a salary increase
Salary increases are discussed during the annual performance review in March, or at a quarterly check-in with your direct manager. Prepare documented results (objectives delivered, measurable impact) before the meeting.

## Deductions from your pay
Your gross salary is reduced by the employee social security contribution (CNSS, 9.18% of gross salary) and by income tax (IRPP), withheld according to the progressive tax scale. The result is your net salary.

## Payslips
Payslips (fiches de paie) are issued electronically and can be downloaded from the employee portal between the 26th and the 28th of each month.
