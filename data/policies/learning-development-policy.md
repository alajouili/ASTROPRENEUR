# Learning and Development Policy (fictional company, 2026)

## Annual training budget
Every full-time employee has a training budget of 1,500 EUR per calendar year for courses, certifications, technical books and conferences. Unused budget does not carry over to the next year.

## How to use the budget
Request approval from your manager in the HR portal before booking. After approval, book directly and submit the invoice through the expenses tool. Travel and accommodation for approved conferences are paid from the travel budget, not the training budget.

## Training time
Employees may use up to 5 working days per year for approved training during working hours.

## Repayment clause
If the company pays more than 3,000 EUR for a single certification and the employee resigns within 12 months, a share of the cost is repaid, decreasing monthly.
