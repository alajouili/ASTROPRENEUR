# Compensation Bands (fictional company, 2026, HR only)

## Salary bands
Junior Data Analyst: 28,000 to 36,000 EUR per year. Data Analyst: 36,000 to 48,000 EUR per year. Senior Data Analyst: 48,000 to 62,000 EUR per year. Offers outside the band need approval from the HR director.

## Annual review
Salaries are reviewed once a year in March. Increases depend on the performance review and the position in the band; the default budget is 3% of payroll.

## Confidentiality
Salary bands are confidential HR information. They must not be shared with candidates or employees outside an offer or a review conversation.
