# Data Protection Policy (fictional company, 2026)

## Personal data we process
The company processes employee and applicant personal data only for clear purposes: recruitment, payroll, benefits, legal obligations and security. Applicant CVs are kept for 12 months after the recruitment ends, unless the applicant agrees to a longer period.

## Your rights
Under the GDPR you can access, correct or delete your personal data, restrict or object to its processing, and receive a copy in a portable format. Send requests to the data protection officer at privacy@trusthr.demo. The company answers within one month.

## Automated decisions
No decision about a candidate or an employee is taken by software alone. AI tools may rank, summarise or draft, but a person reviews and approves every decision about people, in line with GDPR Article 22.

## Reporting a data breach
Report any suspected data breach, such as an email sent to the wrong person or a lost laptop, to privacy@trusthr.demo immediately. The company must notify the authority within 72 hours when required.
