# Benefits Policy (fictional company, 2026)

## Health insurance
All employees and their dependants are covered by the company health insurance from the first day of employment. The company pays 80% of the premium. Dental and optical care are included up to 600 EUR per person per year.

## Mental health support
Employees have free, confidential access to an employee assistance programme with up to 8 sessions per year with a licensed psychologist. HR never learns who uses the service.

## Meal vouchers and transport
Employees receive meal vouchers worth 9 EUR per working day. Public transport passes are reimbursed at 50%, or a cycling allowance of 0.25 EUR per kilometre is paid instead.

## Expenses
Business expenses are reimbursed within 30 days when submitted with receipts through the expenses tool within 60 days of the expense. Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 25 EUR per meal.
