# Resignation and Offboarding Policy (fictional company, 2026)

Content contributed by Member 3 (Corporate Offboarding, section 6.4).

## How to resign
Submit your resignation (démission) in writing to HR, by signed letter or from your company email, stating your planned last working day.

## Notice period
The notice period (préavis) is 1 to 3 months depending on your role level. It can be shortened if your manager agrees.

## Final settlement
Your final settlement (solde de tout compte) includes payment for accrued unused paid leave days and any pro-rated bonus.

## Leaving documents
Your work certificate (attestation de travail) and the clearance certificate (quitus) are issued within 48 hours after you return company equipment.
