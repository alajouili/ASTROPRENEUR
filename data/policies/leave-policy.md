# Leave Policy (fictional company, 2026)

## Annual paid leave
Full-time employees receive 25 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued monthly (about 2.08 days per month). Part-time employees receive leave in proportion to their working time. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year and must be used before March 31; other unused days are lost.

## Requesting leave
Submit leave requests in the HR portal at least 10 working days in advance for absences of 3 days or more, and at least 2 working days in advance for shorter absences. Your manager approves or declines within 3 working days. Leave of more than 10 consecutive working days needs approval from both your manager and HR.

## Sick leave
Notify your manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. A medical certificate is required from the third consecutive day of sick leave. Employees receive full pay for up to 30 calendar days of certified sick leave per year; beyond that, the statutory scheme applies.

## Parental leave
Birth or adoptive mothers receive 16 weeks of fully paid maternity leave. Fathers and second parents receive 4 weeks of fully paid paternity (partner) leave, to be taken within 6 months of the birth or adoption. Notify HR at least 8 weeks before the planned start date when possible.

## Other paid leave
Employees receive 3 paid days for the death of a close family member, 2 paid days for their own wedding, and 1 paid day for moving house once per year.
