# Employment Contracts Policy (fictional company, 2026)

Content contributed by Member 3 (Employment Legal Framework, section 2.3).

## Contract types
New hires join on a permanent contract (CDI) or a fixed-term contract (CDD) for a defined project or replacement. Graduates may start on a CIVP contract: a one-year graduate initiation contract with a state subsidy and an exemption from social charges. CIVP employees can be converted to a CDI on a fast track, based on their performance.

## Probation period
The probation period (période d'essai) for a CDI is 3 months for technical staff and 6 months for engineering leads and managers.

## Ending a contract during probation
During the probation period, either the employee or the company can end the contract with 15 days' written notice, without severance pay.
