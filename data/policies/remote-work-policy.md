# Remote Work Policy (fictional company, 2026)

## Eligibility
Employees who have completed their probation period may work remotely up to 3 days per week, with their manager's approval. Roles that require on-site presence (reception, facilities, lab work) are not eligible.

## Equipment allowance
Each eligible employee can claim a one-time home-office equipment allowance of 800 EUR, renewable every 3 years, for a desk, chair, screen or headset. Claims are made through the expenses tool with receipts. The company laptop remains company property.

## Working hours and availability
Remote employees keep their usual working hours and must be reachable on the team chat between 10:00 and 16:00 (core hours). Team meetings scheduled on site take priority over remote days.

## Security when working remotely
Use only the company VPN on public networks, lock your screen when away, and never print confidential documents at home. Report a lost or stolen device to IT within 24 hours.
