# Workplace Conflict and Wellbeing Policy (fictional company, 2026)

Content contributed by Member 3 (Employee Protection Charter, section 7.2).

## Resolving a disagreement with a colleague or manager
Start with a calm, factual one-to-one conversation about the work itself. Confirm what you agreed in a short follow-up email.

## Confidential HR mediation
If the conflict continues, you can ask HR for confidential mediation at hr@trusthr.demo. The company protects employees who raise a concern in good faith against any retaliation.

## Harassment and discrimination
Harassment, discrimination or abusive behaviour is never tolerated. A report starts an internal investigation immediately. Report it to HR or to the ethics contact.

## Stress and burnout
If you feel overloaded or close to burnout, talk to your manager or HR. The confidential employee assistance programme (up to 8 sessions per year with a psychologist) is described in the Benefits Policy.
