Pure Deterministic Math
Weights are strictly arithmetic (1.0 for met, 0.5 for partial). The language model extracts text facts; it has zero ability to invent or inflate scores.
Knives-Out Knockouts
Must-have qualifications act as non-negotiable hurdles. If any mandatory criterion is missing, the system records it directly in missing_must.
Verbatim Quote Enforcement
Every positive rating must include an exact verbatim substring from the candidate's CV. Claims without direct quotation proofs are discarded automatically.
