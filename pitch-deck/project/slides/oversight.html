Human approval & audit

Nothing is sent or recorded without a person

The Action Gateway

Plain code, no AI. Before it acts, it checks again:

  • a recruiter or HR admin approved it
  • the payload has not changed since
  • the recipient and targets are valid
  • it was not already executed

The audit trail

Every upload, score, approval and email is logged:

  • append-only, enforced by the database
  • each entry hash-chained to the previous
  • any tampering is detected on verify
  • failed actions are logged, never hidden

Roles are enforced on the server: employees see only the policy assistant, recruiters review and approve, the HR admin audits.
